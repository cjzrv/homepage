Abstract

AcciSAGE is a global-to-local framework for spatio-temporal accident grounding in surveillance videos. It uses one open-weight vision-language backbone with task-specific LoRA adapters for temporal, spatial, and collision-type predictions. Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) is followed by error-aware SAPO refinement with task-aligned rewards.

Method

  • Temporal: Ground the accident moment from the full video.
  • Spatial: Estimate the accident center in a localized video context.
  • Type: Predict the collision category.
